Smaller pieces

Each piece can sit alone on any page. Use one script tag per page, after the last piece.

<div data-lightthefire="fire"></div>       the fire and count only
<div data-lightthefire="counter"></div>    a small count with a give button
<div data-lightthefire="progress"></div>   the ride to the Elkhorn
<div data-lightthefire="map"></div>        the 50-state race
<div data-lightthefire="screen"></div>     full-screen fire for lobby displays

After a gift

Set the DonorPerfect form's thank-you redirect to the page that holds the embed, with ?ltf=thanks on the end. The fire then sends up a spark for the person who just gave.

https://www.trlibrary.com/[page with the embed]?ltf=thanks
